📌 1. Struktur Gramatikal Present Perfect Tense

Present Perfect Tense menghubungkan masa lalu dengan masa sekarang. Digunakan untuk menyatakan peristiwa yang telah terjadi di masa lampau namun akibat, hasil, atau relevansinya masih berpengaruh saat ini:

Bentuk KalimatPola Struktur / RumusContoh Kalimat Profesional IT
Positif (+)Subject + have / has + Verb 3 (Past Participle)She has deployed the mobile application to production.
Negatif (-)Subject + have / has + not + Verb 3We have not received the server security credentials.
Tanya (?)Have / Has + Subject + Verb 3?Have you ever worked with PostgreSQL database?
  • Subjek have: I, You, We, They
  • Subjek has: He, She, It

📌 2. Matriks Komparasi: Present Perfect vs Simple Past

Aspek PembedaSimple Past TensePresent Perfect Tense
Fokus PeristiwaMenekankan kapan peristiwa lampau itu terjadi secara spesifik.Menekankan pengalaman atau hasil pencapaian tanpa waktu spesifik.
Bentuk Kata KerjaVerb 2 (contoh: wrote, bought, studied).Have/Has + Verb 3 (contoh: have written, has bought).
Keterangan WaktuSpesifik: yesterday, last night, two days ago, in 2023.Tidak spesifik / rentang waktu: already, yet, ever, never, just, since 2020, for 3 years.
Contoh KomparasiI built this website last year. (Waktu jelas tahun lalu).I have built three e-commerce websites. (Pengalaman hidup).

📌 3. Penggunaan Khas: Since vs For

  • Since (Sejak): Menunjukkan titik awal dimulainya suatu peristiwa di masa lampau (starting point). Contoh: He has studied computer science since 2022.
  • For (Selama): Menunjukkan durasi atau panjangnya rentang waktu berlangsungnya peristiwa (duration of time). Contoh: They have worked at Google for five years.

💡 BAGIAN 2: PENGAYAAN MATERI FIRST PRINCIPLES, INDUSTRI & JEBAKAN UTS

  • Wawancara Kerja IT: Pewawancara sering bertanya: 'Have you ever designed a relational database?' Jawablah dengan Present Perfect untuk merangkum pengalaman, lalu jabarkan rincian teknisnya menggunakan Simple Past: 'Yes, I have. Last semester, I designed an inventory schema for a local pharmacy.'
  • Jebakan Ujian: Jangan gunakan Present Perfect bila terdapat keterangan waktu lampau spesifik seperti yesterday (salah: 'I have seen him yesterday' → benar: 'I saw him yesterday').
  • Irregular Verbs: Kuasai bentuk Verb 3 dari kata kerja umum IT: build → built → built, write → wrote → written, run → ran → run.
