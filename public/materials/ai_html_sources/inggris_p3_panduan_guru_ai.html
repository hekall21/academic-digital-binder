BAB 1. Past Simple vs Present Perfect in Engineering Reports

In technical engineering reports, selecting between Past Simple and Present Perfect determines whether an event is viewed as a finished historical milestone or an ongoing operational state:

Tense Structure Grammatical Formula Time Focus & Temporal Aspect Common Signal Words Real-World Engineering Example
Past Simple Subject + Verb 2 (ed / irregular) Definite past time; the action is completely finished at a specified past moment. yesterday, last week, in 2024, two hours ago, during sprint 3 "The DevOps team deployed hotfix v2.1.0 yesterday at 23:00 UTC."
Present Perfect Subject + have/has + Verb 3 (Past Participle) Indefinite past time with direct relevance to current operational state or unfinished time window. already, yet, so far, recently, since Monday, for three cycles "Our QA engineers have resolved 42 edge cases so far this sprint."
