BAB 1 The Big Picture: Past Simple vs Present Perfect in Engineering Reports

Materi Slide Resmi

In technical sprint retrospectives, incident postmortems, and academic research papers, choosing between the Past Simple and the Present Perfect communicates critical temporal nuances about system states:

Dimension Past Simple Tense Present Perfect Tense
Formula Subject + Verb 2 (ed / irregular) Subject + have/has + Verb 3 (Past Participle)
Temporal Focus Completed action at a specific, closed past timestamp. Action happened at an unspecified time, or extends into the present with ongoing operational relevance.
Time Signal Words yesterday, last week, in 2024, two hours ago, during sprint 3 already, yet, just, recently, so far, since Monday, for three hours
IT Engineering Example "The DBA restored the database backup yesterday at 22:00." "The team has resolved 45 production tickets so far this month."

BAB 2 Regular vs Irregular Verb Conjugations in Software Engineering

Materi Slide Resmi
Base Form (V1) Past Simple (V2) Past Participle (V3) Contextual Computing Usage
compile compiled compiled "The compiler has successfully generated the machine code."
build built built "The CI/CD pipeline built the release artifact last night."
run ran run "We ran the integration test suite on the staging server."
write wrote written "The engineer has written comprehensive unit test mocks."
find found found "The penetration tester found two critical SQL injection flaws."
